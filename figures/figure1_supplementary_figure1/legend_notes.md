# Figure 1 and Supplementary Figure 1: legends (release hvg_exclusion_vbpca_20261012)

Generated by `scripts/make_figures.R` from saved development outputs: HVG–EN from `rebutal4/model_development` and Version B–PCA from `rebutal3 .../model_development`. The script verifies that the locked models in `rebutal4/lockedmodel` are identical to the final fits whose results are plotted. No model was refitted. Panel-level source data is in `source_data/`, and single-panel PDFs are in `panels/`.

Clock names: **HVG–EN (single-age excl.)** = HVG → elastic net, trained without single-age studies; **Version B–PCA** = Version B selector → PCA → glmnet, trained on all studies.

---

**Figure 1 | Study-grouped nested development and locked final models of two ovarian transcriptomic age clocks.**

**a,** Development procedure. In each outer split, whole studies are held out. Within each outer training set only:
- **Training rule.** For HVG–EN, the five studies sampled at a single age are removed: Morris, CRA017492, GSE252791, Zhao and GSE194196 (26 profiles, 7–16 weeks). Version B–PCA uses all training studies.
- **Fold-local features.** Expression is ln(1+CPM). Genes must be detected at CPM ≥ 1 in ≥ 50% of training profiles. N ∈ {500, 1,000, 2,000, 3,000, 5,000} genes are selected, by HVG variance rank or by the Version B age partial-R² selector.
- **Regression.** Genes are scaled; Version B–PCA then applies PCA (1–10 PCs). A study-weighted elastic net is fitted (α ∈ {0.1, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 0.9, 1}; 100 fixed λ from 10³ to 10⁻⁴).
- **Selection.** Each candidate is scored by inner leave-one-study-out CV as the mean held-out-study MAE. λ is the largest value within one study-level SE of the minimum, then the best (N, α, PCs) is chosen.
- **Refit and predict.** The chosen recipe is refitted on the whole outer training set and predicts the held-out studies without calibration.

**b,** Cohort and validation design: 136 profiles from 14 C57BL/6 studies (filled = bulk RNA-seq, open = scRNA-seq pseudobulk; † = single-age study). Right: each study's role at each stage. Stage A has four study-grouped folds (held-out n = 36, 48, 20, 32). Stage B holds out NIHMS1554107 (n = 18), and stage C holds out Winkler (n = 29). The final fit has no held-out set. Single-age studies are held out in their Stage A fold for both clocks, but are used for training only by Version B–PCA (yellow). The HVG–EN final model therefore trains on 110 profiles from 9 studies, and Version B–PCA on 136 profiles from 14 studies.

**c,** Held-out MAE per study. Stage A rows are labelled with the outer fold in which the study was held out; stages B and C each have one study.

**d,** Inner-CV selection of gene count. Each cell gives the inner MAE (weeks) of the best (α, λ[, PCs]) candidate for that N at each stage. Shading is the difference from that stage's best value, and the outlined cell is the selected N. Inner MAEs are not comparable between the two clocks: HVG–EN's inner folds contain only multi-age studies.

**e,** Held-out predicted versus chronological age. Row A: pooled out-of-fold predictions for all 136 profiles, coloured and shaped by held-out fold. Rows B and C: the single-study holdouts. Labels give the pooled MAE (in row A also the mean of per-study MAEs) and the Pearson r. The dashed line is identity. Predictions are uncalibrated and can fall below zero; four HVG–EN Stage A predictions for 7–9-week profiles lie between −3.9 and −0.2 weeks.

**f,** Locked final models. HVG–EN: N = 3,000, α = 0.5, λ = 0.179, 79 nonzero genes; the 20 largest coefficients are shown, in weeks per gene SD. Version B–PCA: N = 3,000, 10 PCs, α = 1, λ = 53.4; four PCs (PC1, PC2, PC5, PC8) have nonzero coefficients, in weeks per PC score unit. Gene-level interpretation of the PCA clock is given in Supplementary Fig. 2.

The single-age exclusion rule for HVG–EN was adopted after the outer results of an earlier all-study version had been seen (Supplementary Fig. 1e). All outer errors therefore describe model development, not independent validation. No in-sample error of a final model is reported as performance.

**Supplementary Figure 1 | Hyperparameter selection, training-rule comparison, stability and error structure of the two locked clocks.**

**a,** Inner tuning curve of each locked final fit for its selected N, α and PCs (HVG–EN: 9 inner studies; Version B–PCA: 14). Line and ribbon: mean ± study-level SE of the held-out-study MAE. Dotted line: one-SE threshold. Dashed line: λ at the minimum. Red line: the deployed λ.

**b,** Final-fit inner-CV landscape: MAE at the one-SE λ for each N × α (Version B–PCA, best PC count shown) and each N × PCs for Version B–PCA (best α). The outlined cell is the selected candidate. Colour scales differ between clocks.

**c,** Inner tuning curves for the selected candidate in each outer stage (A folds 1–4, B, C), drawn as in **a**.

**d,** Selected settings per stage: outer train/test profiles, number of training studies, N, α, λ, PCs, nonzero genes and inner MAE. Version B–PCA models are dense in gene space ("dense"). The final fit is shaded.

**e,** HVG–EN training rule, all studies (previous locked model, open) versus single-age exclusion (locked, filled), on identical held-out profiles:
- **Left: main split and holdouts.** Stage A pooled 16.10 → 9.90 weeks; Stage A study-mean 16.17 → 10.07; B 23.18 → 9.33; C 17.65 → 16.91.
- **Right: the same 30 study-grouped repeated splits.** Pooled MAE median 15.96 → 10.98 weeks; exclusion is better in 30/30 splits. Lines connect paired splits.

**f,** Count tuning compared with the prespecified fixed N = 2,000 reference, each under its clock's training rule.

**g,** Thirty repeated study-grouped four-fold partitions, the same assignments for every procedure. Lines connect paired repeats. The spread shows dependence on study grouping and is not a confidence interval.

**h,** Frequency of each selected N across the 120 outer fits of the repeated partitions. "final" marks the N of the locked model.

**i,** Stability of the final HVG–EN genes across the 120 repeated outer fits.
- **Dots:** the fraction of fits in which the gene's coefficient is nonzero.
- **Crosses:** the fraction in which the gene is in the candidate gene pool (median 0.84).

Nine of the 79 genes are nonzero in ≥ 50% of fits: Igkc and Cd5l 0.74, Col9a2 0.73, Cma1 0.66, Pcdhb9 0.64, Atp6v0d2 0.62, Apoa1 0.59, Lars2 0.54, Grin2b 0.53. The median is 0.10. Elastic net keeps one gene from a correlated group fairly arbitrarily, so low per-gene stability does not imply an unstable age signal.

**j,** Signed out-of-fold error (predicted − chronological) against age in Stage A, with a LOESS trend. Version B–PCA shows regression toward the training mean (young overpredicted, old underpredicted); HVG–EN's trend is flatter. No calibration is applied.
