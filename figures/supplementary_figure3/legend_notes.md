**Supplementary Figure 3 | Comparison of the locked ovarian clocks with tAge for individual contrasts, gene weights and pathways.**

For every clock, the contrast effect is the mean predicted age of the target group minus that of its matched control group, in weeks. tAge outputs (months) were converted to weeks and centred on the same control group, so tAge effects are control-relative transcriptomic shifts, not conventional age acceleration. The primary set has 38 adult, prespecified, non-placement contrasts from 16 datasets. Within these contrasts, target and control ages differ by at most 2.1 weeks. Contrast-level agreement and its robustness (analysis sets, bulk only, training-study exclusion, leave-one-dataset-out, sample level) are shown in the main figure. Effect magnitudes differ between clocks (median |effect|: HVG–EN 5.2, VB–PCA 2.1, tAge scaled-diff 7.8, tAge YuGene-diff 12.8 weeks), so agreement is in ranking and direction, not in absolute weeks.

**a,** Per-contrast effects (weeks) for all four clocks, grouped by contrast type (M = microbiome). Within each group, rows are ordered by the mean effect across clocks. Colour is clipped at ±40 weeks; the printed value is unclipped. \* = nominal two-sided Welch P < 0.05 for that clock (target vs control predicted age). After BH correction within each clock across the 38 contrasts, 1–2 contrasts per clock have q < 0.05 (full statistics in source data). Annotation columns:
- **Mod.:** B = bulk, PB = pseudobulk.
- **Train:** C = control profiles come from a study used to train a locked clock.
- **Cov.:** minimum fraction (%) of tAge model input genes present in the contrast's samples (78–93%, median 89%).
- **Sign:** how the four clocks split by effect sign. 4/4 = all agree (18 contrasts, shaded); 3/4 (13); 2/2 = split evenly (7).

**b,** Gene-weight concordance on genes shared between the ovarian gene universe and both tAge feature maps.
- **Top row:** 74 of the 79 nonzero HVG–EN genes. Coloured by whether the HVG–EN standardized coefficient has the same sign as the tAge coefficient. The eight largest |HVG–EN| coefficients are labelled.
- **Bottom row:** all 2,935 VB–PCA genes, as backprojected weights (rotation × PC coefficient), shown as hexagonal-bin density.
- **Text:** Pearson r, Spearman ρ (nominal two-sided P: \*<0.05, \*\*<0.01, \*\*\*<0.001, \*\*\*\*<0.0001; BH-adjusted values in source data) and the percentage of genes with the same sign.
- **Agreement is weak** at the gene level (HVG–EN r = 0.30–0.35, about 57% same sign; VB–PCA r ≈ 0.05, 51% same sign), in contrast to the strong agreement of contrast effects (main figure). The clocks therefore reach similar sample-level conclusions through largely different gene weightings.
- **Scales:** feature transformations differ between models, so coefficient magnitudes are not comparable across axes.

**c,** Model-resolved pathway weighting for the 15 representative pathways.
- **Pathways:** five per group, ≥ 15 mapped genes, Jaccard overlap < 0.35 between representatives. Selection is by effect size, not by name.
- **Importance:** rank-biserial effect (2 × AUC − 1) for pathway versus non-pathway genes, computed on absolute model weights.
- **Direction:** the same effect computed on signed weights. Red = pathway weights shifted toward positive (older) age coefficients; blue = toward negative (younger) coefficients.
- **Stars:** permutation BH FDR < 0.05 (100,000 shared gene-label permutations; common 12,263-gene background; 6,637 pathways).
- **Right:** ovary-clock mean minus tAge mean importance. This difference is descriptive and is not a test of tissue specificity.
- **Pathway counts:** none of the 6,637 pathways is supported in all four clocks. 40 have support from at least one ovary clock and one tAge variant, 389 from ovary clocks only and 67 from tAge only.
- **Interpretation:**
  - Shared weighting concerns immune and antimicrobial-defence pathways, carried in the ovary by VB–PCA.
  - Ovary-preferential candidates include NK-cell cytotoxicity, the TCA cycle and branched-chain amino-acid catabolism.
  - tAge-preferential candidates are extracellular-matrix programs (laminin, collagen fibril assembly, elastic fibres).
  - HVG–EN, with only 74 active genes in the background, has no pathway reaching FDR support.
